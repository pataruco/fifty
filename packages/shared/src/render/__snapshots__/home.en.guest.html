The route follows the Camino de Santiago. It is an old pilgrim path to the city of Santiago de Compostela.

The route in 4 parts

You can join any part. Come for 1 day or stay for more.

  1. 1. England: Reading to Southampton

    110 km (68 miles) on a path called St James' Way.

    Sunday 7 to Sunday 14 May

    You can join

    Come for 1 day or more. 5 of the 7 days are easy day trips by train from London.

    Message Pedro to join part 1

  2. 2. Ferry to Spain: Portsmouth to Ferrol

    No walking. A ferry to Santander in Spain, then a bus or train along the north coast to Ferrol.

    Monday 15 to Wednesday 17 May

    You can join

    Come on the ferry with me. It leaves Portsmouth on Monday evening and reaches Santander on Wednesday morning. You book your own ticket and cabin.

    Message Pedro to join part 2

  3. 3. Spain: Ferrol to Santiago de Compostela

    115 km (71 miles) on a path called the Camino Inglés. That means the English Way.

    Thursday 18 to Tuesday 23 May

    You can join

    Walk all 5 days, or fly to Santiago de Compostela for the arrival on Monday 22 May.

    Message Pedro to join part 3

  4. 4. Spain: Santiago de Compostela to Finisterre

    90 km (56 miles) to the Atlantic coast.

    Wednesday 24 to Saturday 27 May

    You can join

    Walk all 4 days, or only the last one on Saturday 27 May. It is 13 km (8 miles).

    Message Pedro to join part 4

I fly home on Sunday 28 May.
